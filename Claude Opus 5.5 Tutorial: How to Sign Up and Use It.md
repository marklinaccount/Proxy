## III. Common Claude Opus 5.5 Issues and How to Fix Them

### 1. How Can I Improve Claude Opus 5.5 Response Speed?

In some cases, Claude Opus 5.5 may respond more slowly because requests are routed to an older model when platform risk controls are triggered. You can use the model selector or enter `/model` to switch back to Opus 5.5.

If you do not want the model to switch automatically, you can turn off “Switch models when a message is flagged” in Settings.

### 2. What Should I Do If My Claude Opus 5.5 Account Is Suspended?

You can first export your data and work. Then, use the appeal form provided in the Claude official account interface to request a manual review. Briefly explain your legitimate usage scenario and politely request that your account be reviewed.

### 3. Does Claude Opus 5.5 Have Usage Limits?

Yes. Usage limits depend on several factors, including your subscription plan, conversation length, selected model, and task complexity. Once you reach a limit, you can wait for your allowance to reset or upgrade your subscription according to your needs.

To use Claude Opus 5.5 smoothly, first confirm Claude’s regional availability and account verification requirements. During everyday use, clearly explain your task background, objectives, and expected deliverables, then refine the results through staged reviews and additional information.
